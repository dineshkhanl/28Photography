About Me

Hi, I’m Dinesh Khanal, the photographer behind 28 Photography. I come from an IT background, and photography started as a personal interest that I wanted to explore further.

I’m still growing and developing my photography skills, which is why I aim to keep my sessions relaxed, friendly and affordable. For me, 28 Photography is about enjoying the creative side of photography while capturing meaningful moments for people around Christchurch.

Photo Delivery Information

  • All delivered photographs are selected from the session and checked before delivery.
  • The number of photographs taken is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery count.
  • All selected photographs included in your package are delivered.
  • The stated number of fully edited photographs receives more detailed editing.
  • Remaining selected photographs receive basic adjustments, such as exposure, colour and brightness corrections.
  • RAW files, duplicates, test shots and rejected photographs are not included.
  • Photographs are delivered digitally through a private online gallery.
